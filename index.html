My name is Maxwell Awu — a native of Ghana and a proud resident of the UAE.
Growing up in Twifo Praso taught me that not every dream arrives easily,
and life’s challenges are not punishments but lessons that shape strength.
I’ve learned that to succeed, you must accept disappointment,
believe in yourself, and never lose sight of your “why.”
Purpose is the fuel that keeps you moving, and as long as you hold onto it,
no setback can stop your journey.
